# Qadam product context
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Residents and visitors choosing a short walk in Astana, including people using phones outdoors. Confirmed by the supplied Smart City brief and team UX brief.
## Product Purpose
Turn a natural request, area/landmark and time budget into 1–3 places and an approximate short walking plan. Success is understanding the whole walk and why each stop fits.
## Operating Context
Hackathon design handoff, team of four. This task edits the designer fixture prototype in design/delivery/demo and publishes it for phone review. Production integration belongs to the team.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Three interface languages, kk default then ru/en. Location sent as H3 r9 or landmark identifier. No raw coordinates sent by the prototype. Facts and confidence labels stay honest; only high-confidence open is definite green. Approximate route is not street navigation. Synthetic demo data must be labelled. Preserve existing form fields and API contract. No photos, ratings, favorites or new API features are supplied.
## Brand Commitments
Qadam means step. Existing hexagon logo, cobalt route and yellow center. User approved clearer footsteps, solid route line with steps, and the city-guide improvement plan for home and results on 19 October 2026. UI constraints include a system font and accessible touch controls.
## Evidence on Hand
User briefs in attachments; design/delivery fixtures.json, landmarks.json, existing source and screenshots. Independent critique at .impeccable/critique/2026-10-19T17-41-55Z__design-delivery-demo-index-html.md. No live model/API verification in this task.
## Product Principles
Show a complete feasible walk before detail. Explain why and what to verify. Keep editing reversible and controls understandable. Keep factual values stable across translations.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
44px primary touch targets, readable contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion. Kazakh characters must remain supported.
